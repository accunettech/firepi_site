A simpler device. A smarter service.

More visibility, without more work.

FirePi continues to watch the same critical signal. The difference is what happens around it: device health, centralized history, team access, and smarter notification escalation.

↗

Push-first alerts

Fast mobile push notifications become the primary alert channel. If nobody acknowledges an alarm within the configured window, FirePi automatically escalates through SMS backup.

◎

FirePi watches itself

Cloud heartbeats continuously prove that each FirePi is online and healthy. If a device disappears from the network, the account can be notified instead of silently losing monitoring.

▦

Multiple FirePis, one view

See every monitored furnace or annealer in a single account, with current status, last check-in, active alerts, and history.

👥

Your team, your account

Account administrators can manage users and notification recipients themselves—without changing the physical FirePi installation.

✓

Acknowledge from anywhere

An alert remains active until the equipment returns to normal, while acknowledgement records who responded and prevents unnecessary backup escalation.

≋

A real event history

Review alarms, acknowledgements, recoveries, reconnects, and notification attempts in one durable history instead of relying on individual text messages.

An upgrade, not a different mission.

The core purpose stays the same: monitor the critical Fireye-related signal and get the right people’s attention. The new architecture makes the edge device simpler while moving account, notification, history, and health intelligence into the cloud.

Designed for reliable, unattended monitoring.

A compact ESP32-based FirePi performs the local monitoring and maintains a secure connection to the service. Cloud infrastructure handles accounts, alert state, notifications, and device health.
