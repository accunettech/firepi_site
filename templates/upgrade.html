A few things to know

A bigger picture.

Is VigilPoint replacing FirePi?

VigilPoint is the next chapter for FirePi. The name reflects a move from a local notification device to a connected platform for equipment, device health, and team response. We'll explain the upgrade steps for your installation directly.

What happens if the device loses power or internet?

The cloud can detect that regular device check-ins have stopped and notify your team through its alert channels. Lost contact is reported as an unreachable device; it cannot identify the exact cause on its own.

Does acknowledging an alert fix the equipment?

No. It records that someone is taking responsibility and stops further escalation. VigilPoint monitors conditions; it never operates equipment or bypasses safety systems. Recovery is tracked separately.

What do I need for mobile notifications?

You'll need your VigilPoint login, the companion app on a supported iPhone or Android device, and permission for the app to notify you. We'll provide installation and setup details with your upgrade instructions.
